Problem Statement

Reliable Rural Electrification

Over 700 million people globally lack reliable electricity access. Grid extension is economically unviable for remote areas. Standalone renewable microgrids offer a pathway — but intermittency of solar and wind demands intelligent storage and dispatch strategies.

Solar PV alone suffers from night-time and cloud-cover gaps
Wind resources are seasonally variable and site-dependent
Hydrogen offers long-duration seasonal storage that batteries cannot provide
Lowest LCOE configuration must balance capital cost vs. reliability
02 / 06
System Configuration

Hybrid Architecture

Three primary energy sources (PV, wind, hydrogen FC) combined with battery buffer and electrolyzer for hydrogen production. HOMER Pro performed multi-year techno-economic optimization across 500+ configurations.

PV array: 15 kWp monocrystalline panels
Wind: 5 kW small-scale HAWT turbine
Electrolyzer: 3 kW PEM type for H₂ generation
Fuel cell: 2 kW backup during extended low-RE periods
Battery: 10 kWh Li-ion short-term buffer

Methodology

HOMER Pro + MATLAB Workflow

Site-specific solar irradiance and wind speed data (NASA MERRA-2 dataset) were imported into HOMER Pro. Load profile was synthesized from a 50-household village demand survey. HOMER ran multi-year simulations with annualized cost optimization. MATLAB was used for post-processing LCOE sensitivity and component degradation analysis.

25-year project lifetime, 6% discount rate
LCOE and LCOH as primary optimization objectives
Load following vs. cycle-charging dispatch strategies compared
Sensitivity analysis on fuel cell replacement cost ± 30%
04 / 06
Results & Outcomes

Optimal Configuration

The optimized PV–Wind–H₂ configuration achieved highly competitive LCOE values with 99.6% reliability. Hydrogen storage eliminated 94% of unmet energy events that battery-only systems would experience during extended low-irradiance periods.

LCOE: $0.18/kWh — competitive with diesel genset at $0.28/kWh
LCOH (green H₂): $4.2/kg from surplus RE generation
Reliability: 99.6% annual load coverage
CO₂ avoided: ~28 tonnes/year vs. diesel baseline

Learnings & Future Work

Key Takeaways

This project built deep expertise in techno-economic modelling, long-duration energy storage trade-offs, and the real cost of energy reliability. Future directions include demand response integration and real-time SCADA-based dispatch optimization.

Hydrogen electrolyzer capital cost is the dominant LCOE driver (>40%)
Wind complementarity with solar significantly improves seasonal reliability
Load-following dispatch outperforms cycle-charging for H₂ systems
Next: demand-side management + real-time IoT dispatch controller

Project Timeline

How it came together

Phase 1 — Research
Site Selection & Resource Assessment
Jun 2024 — Jul 2024

Identified a representative remote village site in Rajasthan. Collected NASA MERRA-2 hourly solar irradiance and wind speed data for 10-year period. Synthesized village load profile from household surveys and census data.

NASA MERRA-2 Resource Assessment Load Profile
Phase 2 — Modelling
HOMER Pro Setup & Configuration Space
Jul 2024 — Aug 2024

Configured HOMER Pro with 500+ system configurations across PV array sizes (5–25 kWp), wind turbine options, electrolyzer capacities, and battery bank sizes. Set techno-economic parameters: 25-year lifetime, 6% discount rate, component capital costs from market data.

HOMER Pro Techno-Economic LCOE Optimization
Phase 3 — Optimization
Multi-Year Simulation & Dispatch Analysis
Aug 2024 — Sep 2024

Ran HOMER optimization across all configurations using load-following and cycle-charging dispatch strategies. Filtered results by reliability threshold (>99%) and ranked by NPC. Analyzed hourly dispatch profiles for the optimal system to understand seasonal hydrogen storage dynamics.

Multi-Year Simulation Dispatch Optimization Reliability Analysis
Phase 4 — Analysis
MATLAB Post-Processing & Sensitivity
Sep 2024 — Oct 2024

Exported HOMER results to MATLAB for sensitivity analysis. Studied LCOE sensitivity to electrolyzer cost (±30%), fuel cell replacement frequency, and carbon price scenarios. Generated comparative charts for PV-only, PV-battery, and PV-Wind-H₂ configurations.

MATLAB Sensitivity Analysis Carbon Pricing
Phase 5 — Reporting
Technical Report & Dissemination
Oct 2024 — Nov 2024

Compiled comprehensive technical report with system schematics, optimization results, sensitivity charts, and policy recommendations for rural electrification deployment. Presented findings to academic peers and faculty panel.
