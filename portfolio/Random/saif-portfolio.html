About Me

I am an Electrical Engineer specializing in the intersection of power electronics, green energy systems, and computational intelligence. My journey bridges the physical hardware of fuel cells and EV powertrains with the optimization power of machine learning and HOMER Pro.

Currently, as an R&D Trainee at Denso International India Ltd, I analyze V2X systems and benchmark OBC/DC-DC converters, aligning automotive innovation with AIS-038 standards.

Graduating from Jamia Millia Islamia (JMI) with a CGPA of 8.5, my academic research spans deep learning for Li-ion BMS state-of-charge estimation and techno-economic analyses of hydrogen microgrids. I am driven by the ambition to pursue an MS abroad and contribute to a sustainable, electrified future.
