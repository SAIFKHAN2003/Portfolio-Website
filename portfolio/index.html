About

Engineering energy systems that move us forward

I'm Saif, an electrical engineer from Jamia Millia Islamia, New Delhi (B.Tech EE, CGPA 8.5/10, First Division with Honors), currently working as a Product Engineer in the Core R&D Team at Hasnain Energy Pvt Ltd. Previously, I served as a Graduate Engineer Trainee in the Electrification & Component Engineering R&D team at Denso International India, Manesar (Jul 2025 – Mar 2026).

I've modelled a 25kW Solar+BESS Distributed Control System (DCS) in 4diac IDE with V2G/rooftop solar bidirectional power flow, designed a hydrogen PEMFC hybrid e-bicycle (4x range extension), and led Team Hydro as an SIH 2024 Hardware Edition Finalist for a >50 TPD green hydrogen facility. I've also published a conference paper on DNN-based battery SoC estimation at IEEE IC3ECSBHI-2025 and won the DTU-IIF IdeaThon 2024.

My research interests centre on Renewable Energy, E-Mobility, Digital Twins for Hybrid Microgrids, Vehicle-to-X, and techno-economic analysis of off-grid energy systems, areas where I aim to pursue postgraduate research in Europe (target: Fall 2027).

Techno-Economic Analysis of Off-Grid Hybrid RE for Remote Villages

Modelled site-specific load demand and renewable resource data for 3 unelectrified villages in Madhya Pradesh using HOMER Pro. Compared 4 hybrid RE configurations; selected PV+Wind+Hydrogen Fuel Cell as the optimal net-zero, zero-LPSP solution — achieving LCOH of $3.6/kg and LCOE of $0.5/kWh. Extended into journal paper under review (Int'l Journal of Hydrogen Energy).

Industrial-Scale Green Hydrogen Production Facility (>50 TPD) 🏆 SIH Finalist

Led Team Hydro as SIH 2024 Hardware Edition National Finalist. Designed a >50 TPD green hydrogen facility in Ramagundam, Telangana with 80 MW SOEC electrolysis, 530 MW hybrid RE generation, and green ammonia storage (LCOH ~$2/kg). Built a functional working prototype within 5 days at KIET Ghaziabad featuring solar-thermal CSP heliostats and 3-stage UV/distillation water treatment.
