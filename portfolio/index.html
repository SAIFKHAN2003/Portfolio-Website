I'm Saif — an electrical engineer from Jamia Millia Islamia, New Delhi, with hands-on experience spanning automotive R&D at Denso, power distribution at Tata Power, and transformer manufacturing at Tesla Transformers. My work sits at the intersection where power electronics meets sustainable mobility.

From designing a hydrogen fuel cell–powered electric bicycle to optimizing off-grid PV–Wind–Hydrogen microgrids, I've consistently gravitated toward problems that demand systems-level thinking and real-world engineering rigor. My research on deep-learning–based State-of-Charge estimation for EV batteries was presented at an international conference.

I'm now working toward Finding opportunities in the industry — to deepen my expertise in electrified powertrains, grid-integrated storage, and the energy transition infrastructure that the next decade demands.

Research Vision [EDIT THIS]

[EDIT THIS] My research interests lie at the convergence of electrified transportation and grid-scale renewable integration.
