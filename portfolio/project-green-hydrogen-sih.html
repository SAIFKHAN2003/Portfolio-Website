01 / 06
Problem Statement & Scope

Decarbonizing Industrial Feedstock at Scale

Green hydrogen production costs remain unviable (>$5/kg) when relying solely on grid power and conventional water electrolysis. The challenge posed by the Ministry of Power was to architect a comprehensive facility producing >50 Metric Tonnes per Day (TPD) of green hydrogen with LCOH < $2/kg.

High CAPEX of high-temperature electrolyzers and intermittency of renewables
Storage bottlenecks: compressed hydrogen storage is capital intensive
Water feedstock constraints in dry industrial corridors (Ramagundam, Telangana)
02 / 06
System Architecture

Hybrid Renewable & SOEC Integration

Designed a facility in Ramagundam integrating 530 MW of dedicated hybrid renewables (130 MW solar + 400 MW wind) with an 80 MW Solid Oxide Electrolyzer Cell (SOEC) stack. Waste heat from SOEC is recaptured for steam generation, pushing thermodynamic efficiency above 85%.

80 MW Solid Oxide Electrolyzer Cell (SOEC) operating at 750°C
530 MW hybrid wind & solar generation minimizing curtailment
Integrated low-head water turbine for short-term intermittency smoothing
Target production: 50.8 TPD ultra-pure green H₂

03 / 06
Storage & Water Treatment

Green Ammonia Synthesis & Circular Water Loop

To eliminate high-pressure cryogenic hydrogen transport costs, green hydrogen was converted into green ammonia via Haber-Bosch, achieving a 300× cheaper lifetime storage cost. Wastewater was purified via a continuous 3-stage filtration train (solar distillation → UV sterilization → activated carbon chamber).

Green ammonia storage at ambient temperature and low pressure
Zero-liquid-discharge (ZLD) wastewater treatment train
300× lower capital cost per MWh stored vs. compressed gas tanks
Closed-loop thermal integration utilizing Haber-Bosch exothermic heat

Hardware Prototype

Built On-Site During 5-Day Finale

During the intense 5-day national finale at KIET Ghaziabad, Team Hydro engineered a functional working physical prototype featuring a solar-thermal CSP heliostat array, an alkaline water electrolysis cell, and automated sensor telemetry.

Dual-axis solar tracking heliostat reflecting concentrated sunlight
Alkaline water electrolysis module demonstrating steady gas generation
Automated flow, temperature, and current monitoring via Arduino
