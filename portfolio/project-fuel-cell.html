Problem Statement

The Range Anxiety Challenge

Conventional Li-ion e-bicycles face limited range, long recharge times, and capacity degradation. A hybrid fuel cell approach can extend effective range while reducing peak battery discharge stress.

Battery degradation reduces effective range by up to 30% over 3 years
Recharge time (3–5 hrs) creates operational gaps for multi-trip commuters
No lightweight auxiliary source exists in current e-bike platforms at low cost
02 / 06
System Architecture

Hybrid Powertrain Design

A BLDC hub motor is driven by a PWM-controlled ESC. A 48V Li-ion pack provides peak demand, while a PEM fuel cell acts as range extender via a boost DC-DC converter. BMS monitors cell health.

BLDC hub motor — 250W, 48V nominal
PEM fuel cell — 100W auxiliary source
Boost converter — 24V FC → 48V bus
BMS with cell balancing & protection

03 / 06
Methodology

Design & Simulation Workflow

System modelling was performed in MATLAB/Simulink using the Simscape Electrical toolbox. PEM fuel cell polarization curves were parameterized. A Stateflow state machine managed energy routing (FC activates below 40% SoC).

Fuel cell I–V & P–V characterization modelled from datasheet parameters
Boost converter designed using volt-second balance at 20kHz switching
State machine: FC activates below 40% SoC threshold
Thermal derating model included for Li-ion pack above 45°C
04 / 06
Results & Outcomes

Performance Gains

Integration of the PEM FC demonstrated measurable improvement in total effective range. Hybrid powertrain reduced peak battery discharge rates, extending cell cycle life significantly.

Effective range: 45 km → 68 km (+51%)
Peak battery C-rate reduced by ~22% with FC active
Total system efficiency: 82.3%
Zero tailpipe emissions — FC exhaust: water vapour

Learnings & Future Work

Key Takeaways

This project deepened my understanding of hybrid energy system control, power converter design, and constraints of integrating hydrogen fuel cells into compact platforms. Future work: bidirectional converters for regen braking and HIL hardware prototyping.

FC cold-start latency requires pre-charging buffer capacitor
Hydrogen storage (metal hydride vs. compressed) is dominant mass challenge
Next: hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) testing with physical DC-DC converter
Potential V2G extension using same FC stack in stationary mode

Project Timeline

How it came together

Phase 1 — Research
Literature Review & Concept Definition
Aug 2024 — Sep 2024

Reviewed academic literature on PEM fuel cell integration in light EVs and hybrid bicycle platforms. Defined system requirements, target power levels, and performance benchmarks. Evaluated BLDC vs. PMSM motor topologies.

Literature Review System Requirements Motor Selection
Phase 2 — Design
System Architecture & Component Sizing
Oct 2024 — Nov 2024

Designed hybrid powertrain: Li-ion battery as primary, PEM fuel cell as range extender via boost converter. Sized battery pack (48V, 10Ah), fuel cell (100W), boost converter duty cycle (0.5 at 20kHz), and BMS thresholds using Kirchhoff and volt-second balance equations.

Power Electronics Component Sizing BMS Design
Phase 3 — Simulation
MATLAB/Simulink Modelling
Nov 2024 — Jan 2025

Built full system model in MATLAB/Simulink using Simscape Electrical. Parameterized PEM FC from polarization curves. Implemented Stateflow energy management logic. Simulated BLDC torque-speed characteristics under urban, hill-climb, and cruise duty cycles.

MATLAB Simulink Simscape Stateflow
Phase 4 — Analysis
Performance Evaluation & Optimization
Jan 2025 — Feb 2025

Evaluated simulation outputs against benchmarks. Iterated on energy management SoC thresholds (30% vs 40% vs 50%) to minimize peak discharge. Documented trade-offs between range extension and battery cycle life.

Performance Analysis Parametric Study Optimization
Phase 5 — Documentation
Report & Presentation
Feb 2025 — Mar 2025

Compiled full technical report with architecture diagrams, simulation waveforms, efficiency calculations, and comparative analysis. Delivered presentation to academic panel and documented a future HIL hardware prototype roadmap.
