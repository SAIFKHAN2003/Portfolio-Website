Problem Statement

Challenges in Rural Irrigation & Soil Health

Conventional irrigation in remote Indian farms is highly labor-intensive and dependent on unreliable grid power or expensive diesel generators. Inefficient water and fertilizer application leads to degraded soil health (imbalanced NPK levels) and reduced crop yields. Delayed or manual fertilizer application further reduces overall crop productivity.

High diesel fuel costs for off-grid generator-powered pumps
Over-irrigation leading to water wastage and nutrient leaching
Lack of real-time soil health monitoring (NPK and moisture)
Manual, unquantified fertilizer application degrading soil structure
02 / 06
System Design

IoT Solar Pumping Architecture

An off-grid solar PV system powering a 3HP submersible pump. Integrates NPK sensors and soil moisture sensors connected to an Arduino-based microcontroller. The system automatically triggers watering and liquid manure delivery via solenoid valves based on real-time soil thresholds, communicating alerts via GSM.

3HP off-grid solar PV array with VFD pump controller
Arduino microcontroller with GSM module for SMS alerts
NPK and capacitive soil moisture sensor nodes
Automated solenoid valves for localized water and fertilizer delivery

Methodology

Hardware Prototyping & Shared-Use Model

Designed the control logic using Arduino IDE. Calibrated soil moisture and NPK sensors with laboratory soil test reports to ensure measurement accuracy. Developed a shared-use revenue model to optimize the utilization of the 3HP pump among neighboring farms, lowering the payback period for smallholder agricultural clusters.

Embedded control code built in C/C++ on Arduino platform
Sensor calibration curves verified against standard soil lab tests
Sized PV system for worst-case solar irradiance months
Structured community-sharing tariff model for micro-utility billing

Results & Outcomes

Economic & Technical Viability

The off-grid solar pumping system eliminated diesel fuel costs completely. The shared-use revenue model generates ₹8,000–₹10,000/month from neighboring farms, allowing farmers to recover their initial CAPEX in 18–25 months. Soil moisture and nutrient balance improved crop yield by 15%.

CAPEX: ₹2.2L (fully amortized via shared-use model)
Farmer ROI achieved within 18–25 months
Crop yield increase: ~15% due to optimized NPK levels
